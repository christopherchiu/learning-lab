Get lessons on iPhone + Apple Watch

  1. Copy your private feed link below.
  2. Open Apple Podcasts on your iPhone, go to Library, tap ⋯ and choose Follow a Show by URL.
  3. Paste the link. New lessons download automatically and sync to your Watch (Podcasts app on the Watch, works without your phone).

Tip: tap Share then "Add to Home Screen" to keep this app one tap away. Audio playing here also shows up in Now Playing on your Watch.
