# Welcome to Learning Lab

This is your personal audio feed. Every episode is written and narrated for you by your Learning Coach agent.

## How it works

When you find something worth understanding (a tweet, a blog post, a paper, a thread everyone's arguing about), send the link to your Learning Coach. It:

1. Reads the source itself.
2. Researches the context: the paper or post behind the claim, the replies and quote posts getting traction, and the strongest pushback.
3. Writes a lesson in the style of a good engineering blog post.
4. Narrates it and publishes it here and to your podcast feed.

## What every lesson does

- **Explains the idea from the ground up**, so you understand *why* it works, not just *what* it is.
- **Places it in the conversation**: who agrees, who doesn't, and what's still unsettled.
- **Ends with a few questions** so you can check what stuck.

## Tracking and discovery

Your coach keeps a running map of what you've covered and how deep you've gone, and suggests what to learn next. Those suggestions live in the **Up next** tab.
